## Description (max 4000)
Ratify Music is the social music ranking game. Pick a category, argue your case, rank your Top 5, and find out whose taste matches the crowd.

HOW A GAME WORKS
• The host picks a music category: best albums of the '90s, greatest R&B voices, top hip-hop verses, and more.
• Friends join instantly with a game code.
• Debate first: a debate topic kicks things off, and everyone defends their picks in the live chat.
• Rank your Top 5 artists, songs or albums from the suggestions, or add your own.
• Ratify combines every player's ranking into the official consensus.
• The host reveals the results one by one, with music playing for each pick.
• The player whose list matches the consensus most closely wins.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Standard games: play with friends using a join code.
• Solo play: rank on your own and compare with the community.
• Live games: join real-time ranking battles run by Live Hosts.

HEAR EVERY PICK
Connect Apple Music to play full songs during games and the final reveal. Without a subscription, you can still open any pick in Apple Music.

THE COMMUNITY'S OFFICIAL PICKS
Every vote from every game feeds the global ranking for each category, so you can see what the community crowns as the greatest of all time.

CREDITS AND PREMIUM
Hosting and joining games use credits, and credits you buy never expire. Premium gives you unlimited play.

Make your case. Rank your Top 5. Make it official with Ratify Music.

## Things to confirm before submitting (App Review risk)
1. Digital purchases (credits, Premium, Live Host) go through Stripe. Apple normally
   requires In-App Purchase for digital goods (3.1.1). The external-link exception
   applies on the US storefront only, so limit availability to the US, or add IAP.
2. Raffles and host cash-out are paid features that pay out real money or prizes.
   Apple may treat them as gambling or contests (5.3). Hide them in the app for review,
   or provide official rules and the right age rating.
3. Reward points redeemable for gift cards need clear rules, and the age rating must
   reflect "Contests".
4. Account deletion is in-app (Profile → Delete Account). Keep it visible.
